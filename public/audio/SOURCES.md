# Audio provenance

All bundled WAV and MP3 files in this directory are procedural, original tones generated by `scripts/generate-public-audio.py`. No third-party recordings, melodies or music samples are included. The generator also creates `silence.wav`, used to unlock browser audio. Python 3 and ffmpeg are only required to regenerate files, not to build/run the app.

- `walk-on/intro.mp3`: eight-second original synthesized intro.
- `effects/*`: original hit chimes and short victory arpeggios; match victory lasts 25 seconds, leg victory 3.2 seconds.
- Caller: optional separately installed recordings; no caller files or browser speech are included. See ../../docs/AUDIO.md.

The previous caller recordings, walk-on music and externally sourced effects were excluded because redistribution rights were not established. Do not add them back to this public source tree without the necessary rights.
